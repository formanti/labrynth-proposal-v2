The model

One embedded team.

The engagement starts on whichever option you rank first. From there, strategy and engineering work inside your team, and the backlog moves with what we learn together.

The pod
Two roles, embedded beside your team
Strategist · forward-deployed product
Turns your problems into scoped, shippable work, and runs the weekly sync and monthly planning.
Mason · forward-deployed engineer
Moves one prioritized build forward and shows working increments as scope, access, and review allow.
Plus the AI Employees they deploy into the workflows you would otherwise hire for.
How a month runs
1
Weekly working sync. With your operator and the decision-maker: decisions and demos, not status decks.
2
One prioritized active build. The pod works toward agreed delivery targets and demonstrates progress as it becomes usable.
3
Monthly planning. Each month, we update the backlog based on what we learned.
4
Reprioritize at planning checkpoints. Reorder the backlog or propose work of comparable scope, with impacts agreed before work shifts.
